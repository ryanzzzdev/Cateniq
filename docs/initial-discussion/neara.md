## About the Role

As a Senior Software Engineer in our Digitisation group, your goal is to make building digital twins self-service.

Our digital twins are built from LiDAR, imagery and other data sources using a mix of machine learning and algorithms, and then refined by our global delivery teams. You’ll build the platform that sits between those algorithms and the people who deliver with them. When you succeed, a user can:

* **Run the build without help:** Take the process from start to finish. When a step fails, they can see what went wrong and unblock it without a developer.
* **Fix what the algorithms got wrong:** A clean run doesn’t guarantee a correct model. Users can find where the output is off, fix it quickly and confirm it’s right before they finish. Several people can work on the same model at once without conflict.
* **Know where they stand:** Users see machine time, human correction time, processing errors and output quality at every stage. They get an early forecast of final throughput and quality, early enough to act on it.

Our stack spans a Dart-based client application, high-performance C++/Rust computation and simulation engines, and a Java backend for large-scale data services and APIs. Whether your own background is front end, backend, or systems, what matters is that you can earn credibility with engineers working in unfamiliar territory and know which technical trade-offs are worth your attention.

## What You Will Own

* **The interface between the algorithms and the people who use them:** Model quality, before the model reaches any downstream use case. Surfacing problems clearly enough that non-developers can act on them, and feeding human corrections back into our ML systems as training and evaluation data.
* **The frameworks that let this scale:** Breaking work into standard, repeatable units that are measured the same way across projects and regions, so they can be forecast.
* **Tooling performance:** Our delivery teams work in this platform all day. Load times and interaction latency decide how much work gets done and how good it is.
* **Rollout, not just the build:** Your users are internal teams spread across global regions, and rollout isn’t done until people are using what you’ve built.
